### Abstract

The effective use of communication systems is critical for saving lives and coordinating first responders. As such, many emergencies require the rapid deployment of Emergency Communication Systems (ECS) capable of continuously transmitting, processing, and receiving data. These ECS devices come with high installation costs and are not easily accessible; they are crucial devices for first responders and other stakeholders, who need "as much in-field information as possible to forecast their strategy and operations”. Additionally, real-time, accurate information distribution during an emergency is a crucial component of improving outcomes during events. This device aims to address both of these issues by incorporating a two-part system of inter-room beacons and stakeholder mobile devices running a smartphone application capable of locating the device's position within a building and communicating emergency tasks and action items that can be adjusted by key personnel throughout the duration of an event, as recognized through an automated "playbook" system. Through testing and data analysis, this design has proven to meet all stakeholder needs and design considerations.
